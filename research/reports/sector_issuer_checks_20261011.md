## Consequences for model evaluation

- A model that promotes these names needs explanation and evidence quality checks; these examples do not justify stock-specific tuned penalties.
- Cash-flow metrics should distinguish investment/working-capital cycles from persistent weak conversion. Financial companies require different leverage and cash-flow analysis.
- Dated issuer facts belong in a current review or a proper point-in-time feature store. Injecting today's records into older backtests would create leakage.
- A feature attribution explains the fitted model's mechanics. It is not a causal explanation of a company's future returns.
