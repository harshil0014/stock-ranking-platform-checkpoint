# Commercial benchmark access review, 5 November 2026

The research objective is to compete on Indian stock selection for recurring investments while respecting the professor's factors. A comparison requires identical dated forecasts, stock coverage, decision-time information, next-open execution, holding period, cashflows, corporate actions and costs. Product feature counts and advertised backtests cannot establish a matched accuracy result.

## Official-source comparison

| Product | Publicly described approach | What this review obtained | Remaining input for matched NSE/BSE comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trendlyne | Durability,Valuation,Momentum scores, with daily updates and some intraday fundamental updates | Official score explanation, not bulk historical stock-score snapshots | Licensed dated NSE/BSE DVM scores or an authorized dated export on the common stock universe |
| Tickertape | Performance,Valuation,Growth,Profitability scorecards with additional qualitative cards | Official product description, financial scores identified as a Pro feature | Dated authorized score exports and matching security identifiers, missing coverage explicitly retained |
| MarketsMojo | Quality,Valuation,Financial Trend,Technicals, with highest-rated stocks and additional selected-stock services | Official methodology FAQ, not a full historical ranking panel | Dated stock scores or archived calls with publication timestamps, methodology version and universe |
| Stockopedia | Quality,Value,Momentum StockRanks with performance buckets | Official performance-test rules, including archived scores and delisted stocks, later-price fills and zero modeled trading costs | India-region score history and eligible universe under authorized access, then recompute under the common SIP/cost framework |

No subscription was purchased and no commercial score history was obtained in this review. These four products were reviewed as comparison targets. Numerical head-to-head performance remains unmeasured. Missing forecasts must never be imputed as bad predictions or replaced by a homemade proxy carrying a commercial product's name.

## Fixed comparison contract

Match exact ISINs and snapshot timestamps. Select full feature-time cohorts before examining labels. Report both common-universe accuracy and each product's coverage separately. Recompute sectorTop3,globalTop20,12monthSIPXIRR,goal20/goal30hit rate,drawdown,turnover,liquidity and next-open100bp-per-side cashflows. Show current-survivor historical results separately from point-in-time results including delistings. Use a locked future comparison for any fresh superiority claim. Professor alignment is a separate objective with factor definitions documented.

Historical vendor exports can be informative but do not create a fresh holdout. The repository's existing prospective ledger still records stale October3champion scores as ineligible. This document does not register new forecast streams or alter that protocol.
