## Conclusions that affect implementation

The most concrete concern is execution feasibility, not unfamiliar company names. Frozen company facts classify Aayush, Khazanchi and Trident as BSE M. Their minimum executable lot must be verified before displaying an actionable ₹7,500 monthly purchase. Historical SME periods also matter for stocks that migrated: a present-day mainboard classification cannot validate past one-share SIP fills.

Conversely, do not blanket-exclude former SME companies. The [NSE migration list](https://www.nseindia.com/static/companies-listing/raising-capital-public-issues-emerge-selecting-a-migration-to-main-board) confirms MWL migrated on 18 September 2025 and SWARAJ on 13 August 2026. [CFF's company announcement](https://cffdefensys.com/news/) dated 15 September 2026 reports BSE Main Board approval. Frozen series EQ, BE and B respectively are consistent with these developments. These events predate the ranking snapshot.

The `latest_price` label is misleading for this comparison: the inspected recovery facts have trade_date 18 September 2026; ranking terminal prices are 1 October. Differences such as SWARAJ ₹273.05 versus ₹377.55 therefore are not evidence of a corrupt terminal price. Show the price basis and timestamp explicitly. Independent exchange-close reconciliation of every terminal quote was not completed by this review.

For scale only, if independently verified, at snapshot terminal prices these imply ₹158,943.75, ₹177,650 and ₹225,810 per lot—well above ₹7,500. Historical SWARAJ 1,000-share lot is directly documented by NSE; it must not be applied after migration.

## What low downside volatility does and does not establish

Aayush's 5.12% versus Khazanchi's 15.44% and MWL's 19.50% are computed price-series statistics. They establish observed negative-return dispersion only. They cannot establish executable exit risk, financial resilience, valuation or honest price discovery. The activity filter's positive-volume and nonzero-return fractions are useful checks but cannot measure spread, order-book depth or limit-locked exits. Add evidence flags and execute lot-aware simulations before changing weights. Familiarity or a name change alone must never become a negative numeric score.
