# Final research outcome: retain the reference, do not promote a replacement

## 1. Decision and scope

The strongest defensible outcome is **retention of `rank_xendcg_balanced_blend0.25` as the research reference**, with `stack_breadth_balanced_w1.0` retained as a promising, non-deployable challenger. No newly tested model establishes superiority across the requested return, goal, risk, ordering, stability and company-evidence dimensions. The retained reference itself has important unresolved weaknesses; retention is not certification of investment quality.

This completes the research continuation and its evidence handoff, **not the ambition of proving a universally superior investment system**. There is no fresh holdout, production replacement or remote deployment. No V18/V19 product was created. Existing champion artifacts were preserved. The website source hashes still match the initial checkpoint.

The delivery directory is `experiments/major-research-delivery-20261011/`. It contains the current global and sector Top 1/3/20/100, all 4,436 current records, complete profile/sector comparisons for the reference and stack, worst-query evidence, source-attributed trial archives, and a SHA256 manifest with a runnable verifier. Paths below are relative to the repository root.

Coverage is 797,443 evaluated historical stock/date/profile rows over annual 2020-2025 folds; training also uses eligible pre-2020 observations. The current specialist training set contains 1,115,140 rows, starts with 2016-03-31 decisions and has outcome endpoints through 2026-08-31. The current snapshot is **3 October 2026**, not live prices. It has 4,436 identities in 59 classification groups, including Unclassified: 4,303 usable rebuilt price rows and 133 explicitly flagged fallbacks. Historical profiles are amount1000, amount50000, base7500, lookback12, lookback60, quarterly, target10, target30 and weekly. The delivered current lists are **base7500 only**; the nine-profile historical evidence must not be presented as nine freshly generated current lists.

## 2. Exact retained architecture and challenger

Retained score: `C = 0.75 G + 0.25 R`, where `G = 0.8 O + 0.2 P`.

- `R`: rebuilt LightGBM `rank_xendcg_balanced`, 180 trees, 15 leaves, depth 5, learning rate .03, minimum child samples 200, L2 30, seed 20261010. Seventy dated price/relative/profile features. Its relevance target combines return percentile .5, drawdown percentile .25 and inverse-shortfall percentile .25, discretized into 20 levels. Query weights equalize historical sector/profile groups; inference uses within-sector percentiles.
- `P`: action-guarded `profile_defensive_15`, LightGBM rank_xendcg, 120 trees, 15 leaves, depth 5, learning rate .035, minimum child samples 200, L2 30. Target is .25 return + .5 drawdown + .25 inverse shortfall. Guarded training masks unresolved action outcomes. Its four profile inputs include amount, interval, lookback and target CAGR.
- `O`: frozen `pair__catboost_defensive__ridge_defensive__0.05`: .95 frozen full-coverage challenger + .025 CatBoost defensive percentile + .025 Ridge defensive percentile. CatBoost uses YetiRank, 160 iterations, depth 5, learning rate .04, L2 20, seed 20261008. Ridge uses training median imputation with missing indicators, standardization and alpha 100.
- The nested frozen challenger is `dual_b0.35_r0.15_c0.4`: `.50 B + .35 L_balanced + .15 L_return - .40*(1-reliability)`. Its two LightGBM rank_xendcg models have 160 trees, 15 leaves, depth 5, learning rate .035, minimum child samples 70 and L2 20. `B` is the production-sector score's within-sector percentile when available, otherwise the old high-strength rule; reliability is clipped dated active-volume fraction multiplied by clipped observation-count/120, with the recorded missing-input fallbacks. This is NOT an all-learned architecture.

The exact inherited masks, tie handling and fallbacks are authoritative in `sector_price_rebuild_20261010.py`, `guarded_profile_sector_20261008.py`, `profile_aware_sector_20261008.py`, `score_open_models_20261008.py`, `finalize_open_models_20261008.py` and `full_coverage_challenger_20261008.py` under `research/runners/`. Historical and current frozen artifacts remain in their original experiment folders. These inherited weights were selected in earlier exposed research, sometimes under older selection rules. The continuation did **not** pretend to rediscover them or retroactively impose its mean-XIRR-zero rule on old studies.

The new breadth stack learns nonnegative coefficients on differences between 22 component scores and the reference percentile. Eighteen components are three heads (return, risk, goal) from six families: additive EBM, eight-interaction EBM, ExtraTrees, Ridge, compact TabM and embedded TabM. Four are legacy LightGBM, TabPFN-distilled and CatBoost correction controls. Coefficients sum to at most one; the residual mass returns to the reference. Separate below/above-median-breadth fits shrink toward a global fit by `query_count/(query_count+100)`. Only already-matured out-of-time predictions train the stack. Its 2020/2021 scores fall back to the reference.

Exact annual/current coefficients are in `experiments/sector-specialist-ensemble-20261011/stack_models_*.json` and `learned_component_weights.csv`. Current balanced breadth split is .5472636819. The low-breadth coefficients allocate about 39.08% to the small TabPFN-student control, 19.69% combined to Ridge heads and 14.44% to embedded TabM return; high breadth allocates about 89.68% to that student control. That control itself is a small reference correction: these are **not percentages of independent financial information**. All current goal-head coefficients are zero; other annual fits differ. A learned coefficient is not proof of usefulness.

Official implementations and method references: [LightGBM](https://lightgbm.readthedocs.io/en/latest/Parameters.html), [CatBoost ranking](https://catboost.ai/docs/en/concepts/loss-functions-ranking), [scikit-learn Ridge](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.linear_model.Ridge.html), [InterpretML EBM](https://interpret.ml/docs/ebm.html), [TabM](https://github.com/yandex-research/tabm), [PriorLabs TabPFN](https://github.com/PriorLabs/TabPFN). TabPFN checkpoint licensing/attribution remains with its research artifacts; no blanket unrestricted-license claim is made.

## 3. Individual models, combinations and failed directions

The final continuation tested 12 execution policies, 112 specialist/combination policies, and 48 neural-continuation policies. These are configurations, not independent discoveries. Its three locked finalist groups contain 6, 13 and 5 policies; **all 24 fail the broad comparison gate**. The gate has 1,296 overlapping metric cells per finalist, not 1,296 independent statistical tests. Mean XIRR has zero selection weight in these new development locks; returns remain a reported outcome and non-regression check.

| Direction | What was seriously tested | Evidence and disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Established rankers/linear/tree alternatives | Earlier CatBoost, XGBoost, ExtraTrees and Ridge; 275 open-model/pair candidates; profile-aware and local variants | Preserved earlier comparisons; no universal superiority. More complicated or more local models did not consistently transfer. |
| Learned replacement of inherited fixed weights | Outer/nested constrained blends; TabPFN v2 contexts 256/768, direct inference when interpolation checks failed | All six replacements fail. Small return changes do not compensate for risk/goal regressions. |
| TabPFN distillation | 60 hybrids/controls; 15 teacher contexts; 100 student/control fits; absolute/residual targets, two depths, soft-label mixtures | Small corrections can help some cells; many ties reflect fallback years. No broad pass. Students are not exact neural-teacher equivalents. |
| Pairwise error correction | CatBoost uniform/mistake-weighted corrections, 12 policies and 20 fits | Recent expanded pair accuracy changes from 55.3024% to 55.3073% for a small uniform correction: not material broad superiority. |
| Conservative/conditional experts and goal objective | Sector/profile feedback, shrinkage, delayed feedback, goal-aware labels; ten targeted variants | All ten fail; sparse local feedback and utility/risk tradeoffs remain. No company-specific exception was introduced. |
| Execution/downside specialists | Base 70 inputs versus six extra dated activity inputs; balanced L1 versus lower-quartile target; 28 annual/current fits | No finalist passes. Activity quantile is poorly calibrated: 2025 fraction below predicted 25th percentile is 44.18%, not 25%. |
| EBM | Additive and eight-interaction models, three heads each, compact 15 features | Readable effects do not ensure profitable ordering. Selected return blends regress in 865/885 cells respectively. |
| ExtraTrees and Ridge | Multioutput full 70 features; nonlinear and linear controls; standalone and blends | ExtraTrees raises recent pair accuracy to 55.5243%, but selected Top3 outcomes deteriorate. Ridge contributes to combinations without a standalone broad win. |
| TabM and training-budget follow-up | Official k4 internal ensemble, two 64-wide blocks; 15-feature compact and embedded 70-feature models; 12 then 24/48 epochs | All 42 original family/year sets and 28 continuation checkpoints replay. Longer embedded models improve over their own 12-epoch baseline but increase downside versus the champion. |
| Cross-family combinations | Means, medians, target mixtures, global NNLS and breadth-conditioned NNLS; small/full blends | Breadth stack improves 943 cells, regresses 347 and ties 6. It is promising, not dominant. |
| Global score calibration | Direct global sorting and outcome-free within-sector quantile transport to the reference's score scale | Direct sector percentiles lose cross-sector scale. Transport helps some global lists, but does not repair broader failures or prove global expected-return calibration. |

EBM uses interpret-core .7.8, 300 rounds, learning rate .025, one bag, no validation split/early stopping, minleaf 40, L2 5, maxbins 128. ExtraTrees uses 128 trees, depth 10, minleaf 120, maxfeatures .7. Specialist Ridge uses alpha 1000 with training-only preprocessing. Initial TabM uses tabm .0.3, dropout .1, AdamW .001, weight decay .001, batch 2048 and three-head query-weighted MSE. Continuation restarts AdamW at .0003, batch 4096 and gradient clipping 1 on an RTX3050. Therefore its matched comparison measures a **continuation recipe**, not a clean causal effect of epochs alone.

Matched 24-epoch embedded continuation improves 931/1,296 cells versus its 12-epoch counterpart, but still regresses 740 cells versus the champion. Its exposed Top3 drawdown is -39.50% versus -31.31%; 48 epochs worsen this to -40.15%. Rejecting it is not merely rejecting a model because one small configuration failed.

Fixed-stack component removals return removed weight to the reference, without retraining. Removing compact TabM improves 870 cells/worsens 336; removing embedded TabM improves 578/worsens 712; removing Ridge improves 560/worsens 712. Thus a weak standalone neural model can help a combination, while a nonzero coefficient can still be harmful. See `stack_ablation/`; no ablation was secretly selected from exposed results.

Published work informed adaptations, not claims of replication of published profitability: [tree-versus-neural tabular failures](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/0378c7692da36807bdec87ab043cdadc-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.html) motivated matched simple controls; [ICML ranking comparisons](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/lyzhin23a.html) motivated objective comparisons; [baseline bootstrapping](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/laroche19a.html) and [delayed-feedback aggregation](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v128/korotin20a.html) motivated conservative routing. Qlib/TRA/DoubleAdapt were investigated, **not reproduced**. Breadth routing has none of their claimed guarantees. [Quality Minus Junk](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Working-paper/Quality-minus-Junk) informed a current evidence rubric, not a leaked historical fundamental model. Reinforcement learning/portfolio optimization was not presented as completed: the available environment cannot validate realistic execution and rewards well enough to justify such claims.

## 6. Leakage, robustness, defects and reproducibility

- Annual training endpoints are strictly before January1 of each test year; current endpoints precede 3 October. Training identities, source hashes and model replays are retained. No current fundamental values were inserted into historical training. Company identity is not a new-model feature.
- All evaluation dates are already exposed. Development locking helps prevent additional use of later results within a round, but does not erase years of adaptive research. Date-block bootstraps (2/4/8 dates, 2,000 replicates) and leave-one-year-out analyses are descriptive, not selection-corrected significance. [Backtest-overfitting research](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf) motivates caution; no PBO statistic was estimated.
- Retrospective universe membership and sector labels cause survivorship/classification bias. Corporate-action guards remove known suspect windows but do not certify every inherited return. Whole-share affordability and profile parameters are represented, but spread, depth, slippage, impact, realistic fills and complete delisting returns are not reconstructed. There is no credible cost-aware tradable-portfolio superiority claim.
- Current price-input coverage is not historical point-in-time fundamental coverage. Growth/profitability/valuation judgments remain incomplete. Simply adding more neural capacity cannot supply these missing observations.
- The execution current export had a stale all-missing activity column. Original files were preserved; source-identified replay proved the mismatch; current inference was repaired from saved models without retraining, changing historical predictions or reselecting finalists. All28 model replays then passed (maximum CSV error 1.11e-16).
- All42 specialist model sets replay (maximum native error 4.44e-16). Historical Ridge float32 outputs require original-dtype equality rather than treating shortest decimal strings as exact float64 values. Current rank exports require round-trip float parsing to preserve near ties. Neither repair relaxed exact rank checks.
- All28 continuation checkpoints replay with exact native predictions, verified source identities and cutoffs. The isolated CUDA environment leaves the CPU system environment intact; inherited torchvision has a version mismatch and is not used by these tabular runners. GPU/CPU baseline tolerances and the changed training recipe are recorded.

Final focused regression run: **25 tests passed** across execution-input joins, float serialization, specialist temporal/stack safeguards, score transport, stability equivalence, rebuilt price features, pair accuracy, action integrity, conservative routing, pair correction and TabPFN controls. The single warning is intentional: the SciPy reference reports undefined correlation on a constant-input test case. These tests supplement, rather than replace, complete saved-model replays. Earlier reports retain their own separately executed test counts; they are not added into a fictitious new full-suite count.

The archive ledger indexes **81 discoverable trial tables / 3,175 source records**, including failures, partial tables and overlapping ledgers. This is **not 3,175 unique experiments**. An inaccessible historical pytest temporary directory is recorded, not silently counted as inspected. `complete_discoverable_trial_ledger.json` retains each original record/schema/source hash; `archive_trial_inventory.json` records scope and read errors. Specific reasons for rejected research are in this report and the linked study reports; no old failure is relabeled a success.
