# Implementation supplement, frozen before new performance inspection

The original JSON protocol remains unchanged. This records necessary implementation choices before the champion-versus-alternatives outcome tables are run.

- Six author-code neural architectures are executable in this budget: GRU, ALSTM, Transformer, GAT, HIST and MASTER. DoubleAdapt/TRA and the additional foundation families remain reviewed, not falsely reported as tested.
- Add Ridge(alpha=100) and LightGBM(120 trees, 15 leaves, depth5, minimum leaf200, lambda30, rate.04, deterministic seed20261031) on the same flattened sequence features and context. They use the same mature training rows and full inference cohort as the neural models, without using performance to choose their settings.
- Neural independent-stock models use shuffled1024-row batches; relationship models use full date/profile cohorts with a mature-label loss mask. The recorded implementation freeze recomputes an unfinished first fold after a batching speed correction. No validation or test outcome metrics were inspected to make that correction.
- Portfolio comparisons use base7500, global top20 and top3 per eligible sector with equal sector capital. All9 profiles are tested in the stock-ranking and official-index SIP scorecards. This bounds path-simulation costs without implying that every possible portfolio/profile cross-product was run.
- Portfolio selection uses full feature-available stocks, including unknown future outcomes. Saved tabular fits are replayed on this cohort and matched against original valid-row predictions. Any selected stock without an acceptable path or with an unresolved corporate action blocks the entire portfolio observation.
- Fixed12-month selected baskets, fractional proxy units and next-close execution are research assumptions. Stock paths use recovered provider-adjusted prices; indices use official gross TRI, except explicitly price-only BSE500. Neither is represented as certified net fund performance. Costs are0/10/25/50bps per purchase and terminal sale. Contributions unable to execute within the forward window are flagged rather than silently shifted beyond the horizon.
- IIMA's2025-12 survivorship-adjusted Indian factor daily release is an additional descriptive exposure reference. OLS exposure estimates are not proof of independent alpha. Its retrospective revisions and distinct investable universe are documented.
- Full and sequence cohorts form separate reported candidate families. Both365- and730-day circular temporal-block bootstraps use4000 resamples and the simultaneous maximum across every candidate within the tier. Risk noninferiority is a point gate; uncertainty is reported explicitly, not claimed as a drawdown noninferiority trial.
- Calibration is fixed prequential logistic calibration(C=.1): earlier out-of-time predictions with matured outcomes only, at least2 calibration decision dates and both labels. Previously exposed outcomes may enter later calibrations once matured; no same-date or future labels enter. Early dates without enough mature calibration data are unscored.
- Stability uses consecutive available historical snapshots; this is not daily full-champion shadow validation. Liquidity/market-regime diagnostics use available past features and trailing broad-index returns, not future-regime labels.
- Historical periods remain retrospective/current-survivor. Passing a diagnostic gate does not authorize automatic production promotion or a claim of beating every commercial provider.
- Additional conservative pre-analysis check: retain the registered per-metric95%maximum noise floors, and also compute97.5%maximum floors for the two primary endpoints. The final diagnostic pass uses the latter(Bonferroni across IC/attainment) in both block sensitivities, so the either-endpoint rule cannot quietly double the nominal familywise error. Insufficient block history remains unavailable.
